# Oddments

Living web components drawn from paintings, plates, water, cloth and stone, free to take.
**See them all: https://socialacolyte.github.io/stylefeatures/**

```sh
npx oddments                 # list every piece
npx oddments add koi-pond    # copy any of the 63 pieces into your project
```

Fourteen pieces are also on npm, for use with a bundler:

| Package | |
|---|---|
| [`@oddments/branch-growth`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/branch-growth) | A flowering plum branch, painted in ink, that grows down a long page as you read it. |
| [`@oddments/chladni-slider`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/chladni-slider) | Ernst Chladni's own sand figures, made again as you scroll. The sheet is Tab. VIII of his Entdeckungen über die Theorie des Klanges (Leipzig 1787): twenty square plates, figures 87 to 106, engraved in copper by Capieux in 1786. |
| [`@oddments/crt-ident`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/crt-ident) | Any logo, shown on a colour television of about 1985. |
| [`@oddments/details-wheel`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/details-wheel) | Any set of images starts as a pile at the centre of a pinned stage, spirals out through a full turn, and settles into a ring facing outward like petals; grey to colour as each lands. |
| [`@oddments/drop-cap-gilt`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/drop-cap-gilt) | An illuminated-manuscript drop cap for article openings. |
| [`@oddments/hohmann-loader`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/hohmann-loader) | A page loader drawn as an engraved plate of a Hohmann transfer from Earth to Mars. |
| [`@oddments/mimosa-toggle`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/mimosa-toggle) | A switch that is a leaf of a real botanical plate. The plate is Henry Andrews' Mimosa pudica, plate 544 of The Botanist's Repository (1807), a hand-coloured engraving, lying on the page as a sheet. Its great lower leaf is the control. |
| [`@oddments/pretext-burn`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/pretext-burn) | A handwritten letter burning slowly from one corner as you scroll, the way paper burns: the sheet browns ahead of the fire, a thin broken line of embers creeps across it, behind that the paper goes to glossy black char that cracks into plates, then to grey ash, and the ash lifts away a flake at a time and leaves holes onto the page. |
| [`@oddments/pretext-calligram`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/pretext-calligram) | A paragraph poured into a vessel. The text is set inside the silhouette of a real object, a cut-out amphora or vase, line by line, justified so the edges of the words are the edge of the pot. |
| [`@oddments/pretext-gloss`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/pretext-gloss) | The glossed page: a medieval glossed Bible, a Talmud folio or a Renaissance commentary page, laid out live. The main text sits in the middle in larger type, under a painted initial. |
| [`@oddments/pretext-inscription`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/pretext-inscription) | A Roman inscription cut into a broken stone. |
| [`@oddments/pretext-rain`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/pretext-rain) | A handwritten letter left out on a garden table, and a shower coming over it as you scroll. |
| [`@oddments/pretext-spiral`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/pretext-spiral) | A paragraph wound onto a disc of fired clay, like the Phaistos Disc. |
| [`@oddments/stitch-type`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@oddments/stitch-type) | A heading sewn in silk on linen. The text is rasterised offscreen and turned into real embroidery: satin stitches laid edge to edge across every stroke at a slant, a fine split-stitch outline round each glyph, a dark padding beneath. |

The code is MIT. The paintings, plates and sounds keep their own licences, credited in each
package's `CREDITS.md`.

`packages/` and `cli/` are generated by `build-packages.py` from the collection's build; publish
with `./publish.sh`.
